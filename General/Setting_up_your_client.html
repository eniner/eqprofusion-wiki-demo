Setting up your client

Last updated 2020-11-15

1. Obtain a copy of EverQuest Rain of Fear 2

2. EverQuest Deployment

Once this is done you will have a folder named everquest_rof2, rename/store it where you want

3. Download Profusion Server Files

They are dynamic links, and will update periodically, so make sure to re-download after a patch, or every few weeks.)

4. Create a forum account or loginserver account, and setup your client

5. Create a shortcut to EverQuest that DOES NOT USE THE EQLIVE PATCHER.
