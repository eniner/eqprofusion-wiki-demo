AA Stones

These stones can drop at random from any mob. To turn these in, hail Aristotle in PoK or Nexus. There will be a option that you can click on to turn them in.

  • Green Stone of Minor Advancement

  • Frosty Stone of Hearty Advancement

  • Fiery Stone of Incredible Advancement

Epic Gemstone of Immortality

Epic Gemstone of Immortality are used to upgrade progression items that you get from completing certain tiers. All of the combines are done in a Universal Assembly Crate!

If your MQ version supports LUA's - Gem Combine Lua V2 - excellent for making the gems that you need.

| Earring of Immortality | + | Epic Gemstone of Immortality |

| Perspective | + | Perspective of an Immortal |

| Earring of the Overlord | + | Overlords Anguish Stone |

| Shroud of Kerafyrm | + | Epic Sleepers Stone |

| Mantle of King Xorbb | + | Epic Stone of King Xorbb |

To create the gems that are needed to upgrade the items listed above -

| Epic Gemstone of Immortality | + | Epic Gemstone of Immortality | = | Perspective of an Immortal |

| Perspective of an Immortal | + | Epic Gemstone of Immortality | = | Overlords Anguish Stone |

| Overlords Anguish Stone | + | Epic Gemstone of Immortality | = | Epic Sleepers Stone |

| Epic Sleepers Stone | + | Epic Gemstone of Immortality | = | Epic Stone of King Xorbb |

Moneybags

These items have a chance to drop off mobs if the person with the killing blow has the buff Mr. Moneybags! Rk#. This buff comes from the item Bling Bling:Ring of the Rainmaker and usually people will MGB this buff in Nexus or PoK.

These 2 items are good to sell for platinum.

  • Moneybags - Bag of Platinum Pieces

  • Moneybags - Heavy Bag of Platinum!

What does this item do? / What is this item for?

Bear Spray - Maximum Strength - Aim AWAY from face! - Resets the platinum cost for Cuddly Bear of Mass Rage: Click for Zone-Wide Aggro!

Tome of Power: XXXXX (i.e. Tome of Power: Veeshan) - You need a tome from each tier (ToV, Najena, Hole, etc) for your 3.0. They are rare and dual-wielding classes will require 2 of each tome.

Epic Gemstone of Immortality - Rare stone used to upgrade Earring of Immortality Rk. 1. You need 14 total gemstones to get the complete earring. The gemstones are also used to create Perspective of an Immortal and Earring of the Overlord Rk. 1. Stones and the Perspective are then used to create Overlords Anguish Stone, which will upgrade the Overlord earring.

Unidentified Item - Item that can be handed in to Deckard Cain in Pok/Nexus. He will give you back a random item within the database.
