Getting Started

Updated from eqprofusion.orgLast updated 2026-10-14

Welcome to Pro-Fusion

For Client setup, please visit this page - Client Setup Page

When you first log in, there are a few NPCs you’ll want to visit before setting off on your adventure. Many of them offer valuable quality‑of‑life items and services that make the early game much easier. You’ll find these helpful NPCs in our main hub zones: The Nexus, Plane of Knowledge, and Brell’s Arena.

You should also find an item in your bags called the Little Buddy Confidence Booster 5000. Consider it your lifelong companion — it provides a series of tiered buffs that grow stronger as you progress.

The more tiers you complete, the more powerful your Little Buddy’s buffs become

1) Costco

From this vendor I would suggest purchasing the following :

  • Purification Stone (Radiant Cure clicky)

  • Annulment Stone (Abolish Beneficial clicky)

  • Heros Stone(Charm and Nexus Port clicky)

  • Book of Knowledge (Portal to Plane of Knowledge) (Free)

  • Psalm of Health (Complete Heal clickie) (Free)

  • Shrinkinator (As it sounds) (Free)

  • Fabled Journeyman's Boots (50% run speed)

2) Confucius

Remember the old days of buying or searching for your starter spells or skills? Not anymore, find Confucius!

Hail Confucius

- Click “Skills” to train those

Click “spells” to train spells up to level 70

    - Side note - Once you progress further new spells will be able to be purchased from kill points or from mob drops.  Just pay attention to the wiki for the tier that you are working on.

3) Mel Gibson

Mel Gibson - will offer you your first task (1.0) which is completed in Crushbone.

Each of the progression NPC's have level ranges under their name. Just keep getting the task and follow along. Eventually you will move on to Grendel, Darby, and Conan.

4) Mercenaries

If you plan on going the solo route or not boxing a whole group, be sure to pick up a mercenary. They are obtainable in Plane of Knowledge

You will need to pick up the Journeyman Rnk II Healer merc (tank mercs are somewhat buggy currently). You will be charged 18 Plat per 3 minutes of use as long as merc is up so make sure you are looting! :)

  • Pro-Tip, Hail your mercenary and it will give you the option to Buff and equip weapons. Make sure you tell him to equip weapons x2 to make sure he equips one in both hands. Also Buffs will scale as you level/progress through tiers (Like having the BuffBot with you at all times. You cannot buff while merc/self are in combat. So make sure you inspect buffs before large pulls!

5) Patches Ohoolihan

This is the local buff bot NPC, just be sure to hail him before you head out.

6) Stephen Strange

How strange — I explained how to get your first task but never mentioned how to actually reach the place! Don’t worry, you won’t be sprinting through PoK looking for a stone to click or doing anything that ridiculous.

Stephen Strange (teleporter) will send you on your way!

Hail Stephen Strange

Click “recommended zones”

    - This list consist of the progression path of the task.

You may also pick from a multitude of zones NOT on the recommended list.

Make sure to have Stephen Strange targeted

    - Just say the first letter of the zone and he will give you options.

    - If you know the zone short name he will go ahead and teleport you there

You can also create your on DZ of a instance, as you may have seen while hailing Stephen Strange. You can do this by clicking the option “DZ” while interacting with Stephen or you can issue a command in game if you know the zone's shortname.

Command to create a DZ

createdz ZoneShortName

- Example: #createdz nexus

Once you create a DZ, you will get some text in your chat windows that will allow you to click “I'm Ready” or “Send Everyone!”.

I'm Ready - Will only send you to the instance

- Command that it uses is - #sendmeexpedition

Send Everyone - This will send everyone that is in the DZ to the zone. (Only applies to those that are in the same zone as the person saying send everyone)

- Command that is used is - #sendeveryoneexpedition

You can make those # commands a hotkey for easy use.

Other Noteworthy NPCs

Dwight Schrute (AA Vendor)

Dwight provides a selection of battle‑tested items once you begin accumulating excess AA Abilities. Fact: These items should be purchased only when absolutely necessary. Wasting resources before maximizing your AA rings is the mark of an amateur. And Dwight does not tolerate amateurs.

Below is a list of some (not all — only the worthy may see the full catalog) of the items available from Dwight:

Cuddly Bear (Click him to make the bear ANGRY) - This item will aggro the ENTIRE zone that it was used in, forcing every mob/NPC to start charging your direction! Use with Caution as this item can get a little pricey on uses Upon using the bear you will have 1000 Plat removed from your inventory but will be free for the next 5 minutes, so make good use of those 5 minutes! For every consecutive use there after you you be charged in a scaling increment I.E your second click with cost 1100 Plat, 3rd 1200 plat and so on and so forth.

I would recommend a hotkey that will track your 5 minute timer so you can make the most use of this item. Below is a look at my hotkey using MQ2:

    - Line 1 > /itemnotify ${FindItem[Cuddle Bear].InvSlot} rightmouseup

    - Line 2 > /timer 3000

  • Stat Food/Drink - Cost 20AA for a stack of 20 of Food or Drink.

  • Yo Momma So Fat' Stone - Anger proc aug that cost 500AA

  • Personal Teleportation Compass - Cost 600AA and unlimited clicks. Brings a Dr Strange to you for teleporting.

  • Misc Illusions for 20AA per

The Gender Bender

  • Read the popup message after you hail the NPC****

  • This NPC can change your Race or Gender with a few clicks.

Aristotle

During your travels you will pick up tokens that can be converted to AA's and this is the NPC that converts them to AA for you.

  • Hail Aristotle - this will bring up his dialog in your chat window

  • Click on Sell AA Tokens and that will convert all of your tokens into AA.

  • You can also buy AA tokens if you wanted to trade them to an alt

ReNamer Mk IX

Just as the name suggest - He can help you with changing your name.

  • Hail ReNamer Mk IX

To change your name, while you have him targeted ---

/say Name followed by your new designation

    - Example: /say Name Profusion

Altimus Prime

He was created with veterans in mind — and for those who’d rather not relive every grueling trial on their newest alt. His services offer a swift path forward, but convenience never comes cheap. Those who seek his aid must pay in Timekeeper Credits, a currency he guards with almost obsessive reverence.

Click Here to read the write up that was posted in our Discord regarding this NPC.

Bilbo Baggins

This peculiar merchant exists to drain a bit of the server’s excess AA and platinum, offering adventurers an unusually large inventory bag. Newcomers would be wise to keep their distance. The staggering price he demands is a lingering testament to how deeply the One Ring’s corruption has twisted his sense of value.

Deckard Cain

He collects the item called Unidentified Item and in return he will reward you with a random item from the database.

  • You can turn in a stack of 20 at a time or 1

After the first 50 Unidentified Items are turned in, it will start costing platinum.

To reset the cost, you would need to give Deckard Cain a “The Horadric Lexicon”

    - This item doesn't drop from a mob and it isn't purchased from a vendor. If you are lucky enough to get one, you will get a emote on your screen after killing a mob and you will need to pick it up off the ground.

    - Some people trade other items for these in game since it is tradeable.

There is a chance that you could get a bigger slot bag and many other desirable items with these turn-ins but they are very rare.

    - Most of the time, it's something you will just destroy.

The Converter

  • Converts AA's to Platinum

  • Converts Platinum to AA's

That is all that this NPC does.

Fusion

If you would like to have some sort of Nimbus, please go hail him and try one out!

Corrigan Hawthrone

  • Hand him any of your Token of Discord that you may loot.

  • This merchant sells misc illusion items for the most part.

**Edmond Dantes *****

This is the Timekeeper Credit merchant. Most of the best in slot items can be purchased from him with the Timekeeper Credits.

Timekeeper Credits are earned 1 per every hour you are online. This is why you see so many bots sitting around and doing nothing.

  • You can trade TKC (Timekeeper Credits) in increments of 50 or 500

So you created a bot and you have 50 TKC stored, how do I trade them?

  • Hail Edmond Dantes

  • Click - exchange Timekeeper Credits

  • Click - 50 Credit Token

Welcome to Pro-Fusion

BEFORE logging into game please be sure that you have the most current spell_US file downloaded and placed into the correct directory.

<-- Step One -->

Once you log into game there are a few items you will need before venturing out to get your experience and EPICX loots.

From the vendor I would suggest purchasing the following :

  • Handmade Backpacks (Free)
  • Heros Stone(Charm) (Free)
  • Book of Knowledge (Portal to Plane of Knowledge) (Free)
  • Psalm of Health (Complete Heal clickie) (Free)
  • Shrinkinator (As it sounds) (Free)
  • Journey Man Boots (Free)

Any additional items will be nice add-ons but are not 100% required,


<-- Step Two -->

Once you have completed your purchases make your way to Confucius. He will offer you a chance to improve your SKILLS or learn some SPELLS.


<-- Step Three -->

Before leaving the nexus be sure to visit Mel Gibson as he will offer you your first task (1.0) which is completed in Crushbone. I would STRONGLY suggest new players pick this quest up.


<-- Step Four -->

Once you have done that feel free to make your way to Stephen Strange (teleporter), he has a suggestion of zones you may choose from, all zones offer the same loot tables for their level so it is completely up to you which zone you choose to tackle. You may also pick from a multitude of zones NOT on the recommended list. Just say the first letter to Stephen Strange and he will give you options.

Keep in mind we are small community so if you have questions feel free to ask in OOC, if you ever require assistance it never hurts to ask most individuals will assist you if they have the time to do so.
