Notable Items for Progression

Last updated 2020-04-25
  • Green Stone of Minor Advancement Turn in to Aristotle for 5 AA Points (Hand in 1 at a time or Tell him to Import Now and receive 90% of the value in bags)
  •  Frosty Stone of Hearty Advancement Turn in to Aristotle for 25 AA Points (Hand in 1 at a time or Tell him to Import Now and receive 90% of the value in bags)
  •  Fiery Stone of Incredible Advancement Turn in to Aristotle for 50 AA Points (Hand in 1 at a time or Tell him to Import Now and receive 90% of the value in bags)
  •  Epic Gemstone of Immortality Used to upgrade rewards from T5+ progression quests.
    • Combine Epic Gemstone of Immortality + Earring of Immortality Rk. 1 in a Universal Assembly Crate (don't forget to remove any augment before combining).
    • Combine Epic Gemstone of Immortality x 2 to create Perspective of an Immortal, used to upgrade Perspective Rk. 1
    • Combine Perspective of an Immortal + Epic Gemstone of Immortality to create Overlords Anguish Stone, used to upgrade Earring of the Overlord Rk. 1
    • Combine Overlords Anguish Stone + Epic Gemstone of Immortality to create Epic Sleepers Stone, used to upgrade Shroud of Kerafyrm Rk. 1.
  • Moneybags - Bag of Platnium Pieces Make sure you get the Bling Bling:Ring of the Rainmaker Buff when you see somone casting it! These can only be obtained if somone WITH the buff has landed the killing blow on a monster!
  •  Moneybags - Heavy Bag of Platnium Make sure you get the Bling Bling:Ring of the Rainmaker Buff when you see somone casting it! These can only be obtained if somone WITH the buff has landed the killing blow on a monster!
  •  Unidentified Item - Turn these in to Deckhard Cain in Plane of Knowledge, when turning these in you have a chance to receive ANY item located in the database this includes trash!
  •  Tomes of Power
