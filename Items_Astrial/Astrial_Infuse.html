Astrial Infuse

Updated from eqprofusion.orgLast updated 2026-10-14

Quest Giver: Astrial Infuse

Quest Name: N/A

Question Location: Temple of Veeshan

The Astrial Infuse quest was introduced 3/28/2020.

Enine posted the following information on Discord:

The first edition of the Astrial Aug has been released speak to Astrial infuse inside of Temple of Veeshan for information of obtaining it. Let it be known this will not be a walk in the park farm for these items they will take effect. Once the weapon is fully completed by a player then Timekeeper Credits weapons will have a increase in power as well. I will work on implementing the next 2 editions and the final combine today or tomorrow.

Locate Astrial Infuse at the zone-in of Temple of Veeshan.

You say, 'Hail, Astrial Infuse'

Greetings, my name is Astrial Infuse. I can give you information on gaining your Astrial Infuse aug to increase your Astrial weapon stats and damage

I will need you to collect items. These items are Left Corner of Astrial, Right Corner of Astrial, Top Piece of Astrial, Bottom Piece of Astrial, Center Piece of Astrial. Once collected combine them into a Forge for your augment.

Collect:

Left Corner of Astrial

Right Corner of Astrial,

Top Piece of Astrial,

Bottom Piece of Astrial,

Center Piece of Astrial

Reward:

Astrial's Infuse
