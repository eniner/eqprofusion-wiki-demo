Tier 8 Gear - Sleeper's Tomb

Updated from eqprofusion.orgLast updated 2026-10-14

Progression Task - “Raid Sleepers Tomb”

Task Giver - Darby in Nexus or PoK

You will need to kill and loot an item from 6 different named in the zone.

  • The Final Arbiter = The Arbiter's Heart

  • The Progenitor = Proof of the Progenitor's End

  • Nanzata the Warder

  • Tukaarak the Warder

  • Hraashna the Warder

  • Ventani the Warder

Combine all 6 items in a Universal Assembly Crate and then hand in the new item to Borat in Sleepers.

Reward - Shroud of Kerafyrm Rk 1

You are now able to level to 71.

Moving onward - Tier 9 - Xorbb

*After you kill all the warders Kerafyrm does spawn but you do not have to kill him for progression. He also drops 3.0 epics.

Upgrading “Shroud of Kerafyrm Rk 1 +”

To upgrade this item you need to combine it with a Epic Sleepers Stone

Below is the breakdown of creating a Epic Sleepers Stone. All of these combines are in a Universal Assembly Crate

| Epic Gemstone of Immortality | + | Epic Gemstone of Immortality | = | Perspective of an Immortal |

| Perspective of an Immortal | + | Epic Gemstone of Immortality | = | Overlords Anguish Stone |

| Overlords Anguish Stone | + | Epic Gemstone of Immortality | = | Epic Sleepers Stone |

Borat (Kill Points etc..)

The only thing that you can buy with Sleepers Kill Points is augs. They are 30 kill points each.

Epic Enhancement Rk 5 can be obtained from here as well. To get the Rk 5 enhancement you will need to collect 3 items from this zone and return them to Borat, along with your Epic Enhancement Rk 4 aug.

  • Ancient Prismatic Stone

  • Warders Guise

  • Sample of Water from the Prison

Don't forget when you go to hand in those 3 items that you are also including the Epic Enhancement Rk 4 aug as well.
