Tier 10 Gear - Devastation

Updated from eqprofusion.orgLast updated 2026-10-14

Progression

You will need to loot the following items. These drop from trash within the zone. Once you have all 8 of these in your inventory, move your Underfoot Touched Hero Stone to your bags as well. Then hail Borat, click charm and then you should have a option to click ** combine** next to whichever rank you are creating. You won't be able to upgrade the Underfoot Touched Hero Stone if the item isn't in your inventory.
