Tier 14 - Plane of Hate

New from eqprofusion.orgLast updated 2026-10-14

Progression “I Really Hate You Sometimes”

Task Giver - Conan in Nexus or PoK

  • Go to Plane of Hate and talk to Borat

  • Kill and loot the items from the Named listed in the table below

  • Combine the 10 items you collected in a Universal Assembly Crate to create Urn of Binding Hate

  • Give the Urn of Binding Hate to Borat

Reward Items:

  • Girdle of Pure Light

  • Calming Shard of Decreased Rage (Decrease hate aug for belt)

  • Hateful Shard of Increased Rage (Increase hate aug for belt)

| Mob to Kill | Item to Loot from that Mob |

| Ashenbone Broodmaster | Charred Broodbone |

| Avatar of Abhorrence | Core of Living Abhorrence |

| Coercer T'vala | Barbed Sigil of Domination |

| Grandmaster R'Tal | Scroll of Merciless Doctrine |

| Vicar M'Kari | Blasphemous Reliquary |

| Lord of Ire | Heart of Smoldering Ire |

| Lord of Loathing | Phylactery of Absolute Loathing |

| Magi P'Tasa | Focus of Malevolent Will |

| Master of Spite | Shard of Endless Spite |

| Mistress of Scorn | Veil of Withering Scorn |

Upgrading - Girdle of Pure Light

You will need to hand your Girdle of Pure Light + Shard of Blinding Light to Borat in Plane of Hate

Shard of Blinding Light - Not sure what loot table these are on. (Seems to be some sort of global loot table)

- You will need 20 Shard's to fully upgrade this belt.
