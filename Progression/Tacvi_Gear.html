Raid Gear - Tacvi, Seat of the Slaver

Updated from eqprofusion.orgLast updated 2026-10-14

The Fights -

Pixtt Xxeric Kex -

50% spawns 4x adds

- Grab aggro on adds and continue to kill her. (Yes she heals like 20% when she gets low)

- Each add respawns 2 times on death

Pixtt Kretv Krakxt - Goes inactive at certain % and spawns reflections

  • 90% - 4 Hatchlings

  • 70% 5 Hatchlings

  • 50% 6 Hatchlings

  • 30% 7 Hatchlings

  • 10% 4 Hatchlings but Pixtt stays active

Pixtt Riel Tavas -

  • At 30% she will continuously spawn the construct adds

Kill Pixtt and ignore the adds

- When the adds die they don't blow up like they did on live. They just punt ya around

Zun'Muram Kvxe Pirik - also known as the balancer mob

Attack him and when he goes invul, kill the balancers.

- After that you just get to sit and wait until he is no longer invul.

Zun'Muram Shaldn Boc - Tank and Spank

Zun'Muram Mordl Delt -

  • 90% 2x minis spawn

  • 70% 3x minis spawn

  • 50% 4x minis spawn

  • 30% 2x minis spawn but the named stays up and you can kill him.

Zun'Muram Yihst Vor - Tank and Spank

Fake Tunat - Tank and Spank

Real Tunat -

  • Changes appearances

  • First appearance change comes with 4 adds.

  • Then again around 10-20% he will spawn 2 more adds

  • Basically tank and spank

BiS Belt - Girdle of the Fusions Tacvi

Girdle of the Fusions Tacvi is a rare drop off of any named in the zone.

This belt can be upgraded with an even more rare drop from the zone called Venerable Tacvi Book

  • Combine the Girdle and Book in a Universal Assembly Crate to upgrade it

The fully upgraded belt = Girdle of the Fusions Tacvi - Complete
