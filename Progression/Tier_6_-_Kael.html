Tier 6 Progression - Kael

Updated from eqprofusion.orgLast updated 2026-10-14

Progression Task - “A Change of Perspective”

Kael- Ahh The easy part! (Only kidding) Now's the time to start bringing your A-Game! Bosses have a TON of HP! and hit REALLY hard compared to MPG! Kael is simple (Kinda) Kill 6 bosses loot their items and combine in Assembly kit and turn into Borat. You will need the following items:

  • Mindset of Tormax- From King Tormax

  • Sensibility of Keldor - From Keldor Dek'Torek

  • Viewpoint of The Vindicator- From Derakor The Vindicator!

  • Statue of Rallos Zek- From Statue of Rallos Zek- He spawns the Idol

  • Temperament of the Idol- From Idol of Rallos Zek- He spawns Avatar of War

  • Attitude of Avatar- From Avatar of War

Combine all 6 in an universal assembly crate to create a Mixed Bag of Opinions!

Turn in the bag to Borat (In Kael) to receive a Perspective - Rk1

Moving onward - Tier 7 - Anguish

Upgrading “Perspective - Rk1 +”

  • To upgrade this item you need to combine 2 Epic Gemstone of Immortality (not stacked) in a Universal Assembly Crate to create a Perspective of an Immortal

  • Combine Perspective of an Immortal + Perspective - Rk1 in a Universal Assembly Crate.

  • Repeat this process until you have the maxed item called - A Great Perspective
