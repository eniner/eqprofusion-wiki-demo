Tier 12 - Shard's Landing

New from eqprofusion.orgLast updated 2026-10-14

Progression “Did you just Shard?!”

Task Giver - Conan in Nexus or PoK

  • Go to Shards Landing and hail Borat

Talk to Overseer Drent about the Shards Trinket

- Get the “Shards Trinket” task from Drent

Once you complete the task “Shards Trinket” and max faction, return to Borat and hail him.

- FYI - You must have the Shards Trinket in your inventory for the task to complete. If you already equipped it, you will need to remove it.

You start out at -2000 faction and you need to get it to 2000. The easiest way is to convert kill points into faction. Borat can do this for you.

  • Borat will exchange 2 kill points for 1 faction.

Overseer Drent - He gives the task “Shards Trinket”. He wants the following items and they drop off any mob in the zone. You can turn the books into him 1 at a time to get credit.

  • Legends Notebook

  • Legends Book of Shard's Landing

  • Legends Blood Book

  • Legends Scrap Book

Reward for turning in all 4 books - Shards Trinket

  • The Shards Trinket item gains stats as you increase your faction.

Harbinger Glask wants :

  • 4 - Blessing of Shard's Landing

Turn those in to him to receive Shard's Blessing

Shards Cleanser: Gives the task “Shards Cleanse”.

  • Kill 500 of each mob type and 500 named.

Reward: Stone of the Shards Cleanse

Moving onward - Tier 13 - Beast Domain

Armor Drops

Fear Touched armor drops from mobs within the zone. If you want to make the item you will need to buy the following for whichever class you want to make. In the example that I'm going to list, I want to make something for a Shadowknight.

Items are purchased from Master Armorer Synlaris for 126k platinum per. The box does not get consumed on combine but the Essence does.

  • Rust-Tinged Box of Fear [Shadowknight]

  • Essence of Augmentor

From there you put the Essence of Augmentor and the Fear Touched pattern that you wanted to make in the Rust-Tinged Box to create the item.

Recipe link for Boreal Breastplate of the Revenant

Example of Gear - Boreal Breastplate of the Revenant

*As a side note, there is some other armor that drops in the zone if you choose to wear it. Those items just don't have clicky effects

Spells

Level 76-80 Spells drop from any mob in this zone.

  • They are tradeable!

Tradeskill Food/Drink

Zone drop items needed for this would be

  • Planar Energy Shard

  • Planar Goo

These items can be used to create the following

  • Planar Donuts

  • Merchant's Brew
