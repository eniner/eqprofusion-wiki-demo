Tier 15 - Tower of Frozen Shadow

Last updated 2026-10-14

Progression - “Tower of Frozen Shadow”

Tier 15 is built around the Frozen Satchel, a bag you upgrade from 10 slots all the way to the 50-slot Mastercraft Frozen Satchel. You upgrade it by crafting (safe) or by gambling (fast, but your bag can be destroyed). A hidden treasure waits for those who reach Mastercraft.

Requirements

  • To complete the tier: 1 Mastercraft Frozen Satchel (50 slots, Item #1011886) plus the hidden secret items.
  • You cannot loot the hidden items until you have completed at least 1 Mastercraft bag.
  • The tier flag unlocks only once you have both the bag and the secret items.

The NPCs

  • Zone Guide - hail for a popup with the full zone guide. Say a bag name (frozen satchel, normal frozen, excellent frozen, superb frozen, mastercraft frozen) to hear its item ID and slot count.
  • Frostbound Archivist - riddle quest, starter kit, token exchange and the safe crafting upgrades.
  • The Gambler (Mirror Room) - turns Vouchers into gambling credits and gambles your bags.
  • Aristotle's “smarter brother” - sells Vouchers for AA points.
  • Zotro - named by the guide as the source of your starter Frozen Satchel (10 slots).

Step 1 - The Frostbound Archivist's riddle

Hail the Frostbound Archivist and follow the conversation:

  1. Hail - he asks what the ancient cipher [means].
  2. means - he recites the riddle and asks for the [two methods].
  3. two methods - choose the [gambling path], the [crafting path], or [both].
  4. both - he hints at a [hidden treasure].
  5. hidden treasure - he describes the ultra-rare hidden item and asks if you are [ready].
  6. ready - you receive the Frozen Satchel Zone Guide (Item #1011887) and 5 Frozen Zone Tokens (Item #1011888). Once per character.

Step 2 - Get Vouchers

Vouchers (Frozen Voucher, Item #1011882) pay for crafting upgrades and gambling credits.

MethodNPCCostYou get
Token exchangeFrostbound Archivist4 Frozen Zone Tokens per voucher (hand in several sets at once)1 Voucher per 4 tokens + 500 exp per voucher

Frozen Zone Tokens drop from mobs in the zone. Named bosses have a higher token drop chance.

Note: Hand in tokens on their own. If you mix tokens with Vouchers, Leather or Runes, the Archivist gives everything back.

Step 3b - The Gambler's Path (risky)

  1. Hail The Gambler and say gamble to see the options (What is gambling here?, How do I gamble?, What are the odds?, Buy 1/5/10 Credits).
  2. Hand him Frozen Vouchers: each voucher becomes 1 gambling credit. Platinum is no longer accepted and is handed back.
  3. Hand him 1 bag to gamble. Each roll uses 1 credit.

Bags he accepts: 18-slot Frozen Satchel (#1011893), Normal Frozen Satchel (#1011883, 20 slots), Excellent Frozen Satchel (#1011884, 30 slots), Superb Frozen Satchel (#1011885, 40 slots), Mastercraft Frozen Satchel (#1011886, 50 slots).

Important: every roll uses the same odds no matter which bag you hand in. The result replaces your bag, so gambling a Superb can come back as a Normal. Wins and losses are announced zone-wide.

The Hidden Treasure

  • Requires: Mastercraft Frozen Satchel (50 slots).
  • Location: the deepest ice cave.
  • Hint 1: look for see-invis walls.
  • Hint 2: solve the 4 Frozen Runes puzzle (the order is in an ancient tome).
  • Hint 3: the hidden chest requires a 50-slot bag to loot.
  • Reward: a powerful legendary clicky.
