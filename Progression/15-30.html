The Ruins of Old Guk (gukbottom)

Get the task for your Epic 1.5 before coming here!

This zone is pretty simple, but you can get lost in here. You will be on the undead side of guk so mem your undead spells! Just murder anything/everything you can. Mobs do root so keep that in mind, and keep on eye on wizards there is plenty of them. As always stay buffed, don't pull more then you can't handle, and you shouldn't have much of an issue.
