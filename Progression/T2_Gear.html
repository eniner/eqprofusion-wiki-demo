Tier 2 Gear - Riftseekers' Sanctum

Updated from eqprofusion.orgLast updated 2026-10-14

Progression Task “Eternity in Riftseekers!”

Task is picked up from Grendal in Nexus or PoK.

  • Kill Chailak and loot the *book with missing pages *(This is the container for the other items that you are about to loot)

  • Kill Feratha and loot Eternity Binding Glue

  • Kill Ruiner Isaka and loot the missing page

  • Kill Elite Aneuk Miseryfiend and loot the missing page

  • Kill Frostkeeper Rybah and loot the missing page

  • Kill Gelidran Conjurer and loot the missing page

  • Kill Pyrilen Researcher and loot the missing page

  • Kill Girplan Stonesinger and loot the missing page

  • Kill Pyrilen Summoner and loot the missing page

  • Kill Mistwalker and loot the missing page

Combine the 8 pages with the glue inside of the book that you looted from Chailak.

  • Deliver the Completed Instructions book back to Grendal

Upgrading Eternity Loop -

  • Visit Dwight Schrute in Nexus or PoK

  • Hand him your Eternity Loop to upgrade it. (Remove your augs)

Once you have upgraded the Eternity Loop to a Sealed Eternity Loop you will want to visit **Grendal **to get the quest again.

- **Do NOT create the 2nd Eternity Loop until you have completely upgraded the first one.**

Collect all of the items needed again to create your 2nd Eternity Loop.

- Go through the same process as you did to upgrade the first one.

Notes:

  • Each page is lore

  • Chailak and Feratha drop 4 or 5 of their item that you need per kill

  • The other named drop 2 pages per kill

  • Each named drops a specific page tied to them only.

Astrial Greater Infuse Augmentation

While you are making your way through this tier, you will want to collect the Shards of Astrial to make this augment. This will be 1 of the augments that you will use on your Astrial Weapon.

Collect:

Bottom Shard of Astrial

Center Shard of Astrial

Left Shard of Astrial

Right Shard of Astrial

Top Shard of Astrial

Combine these items in Plane of Knowledge using a Forge.

Reward:

Astrial's Greater Infuse
