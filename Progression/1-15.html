Level 01 to 15

Last updated 2020-04-25

Hello and welcome to ProFusion server!

This server is fun and streamlined to keep you busy but not overwhelmed. Everything can be accomplished solo with effort and ability to focus on what is needed. That being said, as a newcomer to PorFusion there are a few things we want to point out to begin your journey through this awesome progression style server. Remember that most everything has been customized and tuned for solo play but boxing is allowed without any limits.

First, being level 1, you will appear in the Nexus and want to see Costco (Newbie Vendor) to grab a few things that will help you on your journey. Simply right-click on him to begin browsing his wares. If you left-click on the Qty to filter out what he naturally sells you will see a few items that are free to purchase. You will want to buy a few bags first. The dark blue hand made backpacks are nice being 10 slots and 10% weight reduction. Next you will want to buy 1 of each of the following,

Universal Assembly Crate (Used for combining items for progression and upgrades)

Little Buddy Confidence Booster 5000 (Clickable item casting a Damage Shield, Regen, and a Heal focus)

Purification Stone (Clickable item used to cure most illnesses & debuffs)

Hero Stone (Newbie charm item with nice stats and 36% haste along with a clicky to gate back to the Nexus)

Annulment Stone (Clickable item used to strip mobs of their buffs)

After getting those items look at the other items he has as some are still useful but not truly needed to progress. Items like Weapons, Food, Clickable buffs like DMF and Shrink.

Also nearby Costco are a few other NPCs. Harold, who gives server information and worth a look at. Confucius, who helps with training skills and learning spells as you level, and Gordon a Banker.

Now that you are ready to begin your journey you will want to first talk to Mel Gibson. Mel gives you tasks to complete that range from level 1 up to level 50. Simply hail him and follow his script. The first task he has for you is to get your Epic 1.0! Yes, you can start your epic at level 1!!. Don't let this confuse you as you will later receive tasks to get your 1.5, 2.0 and 3.0 epics as you progress and level! As for now you simply follow his script to receive your first task and your first journey onto this awesome and wonderful server!

Crushbone will be the first zone you will begin with. You will want to run up the stairs to the teleport platform and talk to Stephen Strange. He can port you anywhere but will list recommended zones for progression. Just hail him and follow his script. Once in Crushbone make sure to "crush" those orcs and get a few levels and gear to be able to fight the mobs needed to complete you task. Gear will drop in every progression zone. It's random drops for all classes so just kill orcs until you get most of your gear and be at least level 10 to 12 to start killing The Taskmaster, The Orc Trainer, and the mighty Emperor Crush! Remember to always check your tasks (Alt+Q) for the required creatures and items needed to complete your task.

We really hope you enjoy your time on Profusion and if you ever need help please feel free to ask in /ooc as a lot of players will be more then happy to assist you.
