Tier 7 Gear - Anguish

Updated from eqprofusion.orgLast updated 2026-10-14

Progression Task - “Overrun the Overlord!”

Task Giver - Darby in Nexus or PoK

Kill and loot a Mark from each of the following named

  • Keldovan

  • Jelvan

  • Ture

  • Hanvar

  • Arch Magus

  • OMM

Combine all 6 marks in a Universal Assembly Crate. This will create a Mark of Completion that you need to give to Borat in Anguish.

Reward - Earring of the Overlord Rk1

Moving onward - Tier 8 - Sleeper's Tomb

Upgrading - “Earring of the Overlord Rk1 +”

To upgrade this item you need to combine it with a Overlords Anguish Stone in a Universal Assembly Crate.

Below is the breakdown of creating the Overlords Anguish Stone. All of these combines are in a Universal Assembly Crate

| Epic Gemstone of Immortality | + | Epic Gemstone of Immortality | = | Perspective of an Immortal |

| Perspective of an Immortal | + | Epic Gemstone of Immortality | = | Overlords Anguish Stone |
