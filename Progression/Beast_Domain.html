Tier 13 - Beast Domain

New from eqprofusion.orgLast updated 2026-10-14

Progression “Beast Reality?!”

Task Giver - Conan in Nexus or PoK

  • Go to Beast Domain and talk to Borat

  • Talk to Velgrath Ironhide to learn about T13 armor.

  • Talk to The Sklyg to learn about upgrading your Underfoot Charm

  • Upgrade your Charm, Acquire T13 BP and Legs, then hail borat to complete T13!

**Note - When you go back to Hail Borat to complete T13, you will need to have the T13 BP, Legs, and Charm in your inventory. Do not have it equipped. **

Upgrading - Underfoot Touched Hero Stone

There are a few NPC's around the safe spot when you zone in that you will need to get the task and turn in the items that they seek.

Jheron Welson has task to find and turn in these items

  • Heartstone of Earth

  • Tides of Destiny Essence

  • Whisper of Stars

  • Beast Fang

Jayt Steed has task to find and turn in these items

  • Elemental Essence

  • Enchanted Wood

  • Mystic Water Essence

  • Eternal Flame

  • Echo of Air

Rocher Hampstin has task to find and turn in these items

  • Sacred Ore

  • Crystal of Unity

  • Spirit Flower

  • Tome of Secrets

The Sklyg has task to find and turn in these items

  • Forest Guardian's Sigil

  • Shard of Sacred Metal

  • Ancient Tablet

Each time that you turn in an item, you get a item back that will be used for either Combine 1 or Combine 2.

After you do the “combine 1” part, you will also get a Rk10 Charm.

| Combine 1 = | Metal Core | Guardian's Sigil | Ancient Tablet | Crystal Shards of Unity | Leaves of Spirit Flower | Tomes of Hidden Secrets | Essences of an Old Elemental | Sacred Ore |

| Combine 2 = | Pieces of Enchanted Wood | Essences of Mystic Water | Pieces of the Echoes of Air | Shard of the Heartstone of Earth | Destiny Essence | Fangs of Beasts | Stars Whisper | Pieces of the Eternal Flame |

The final combine will be the following in a Universal Assembly Crate

  • Underfoot Touched Hero Stone Rk 10

  • Underfoot rk. 11 combine 1

  • Underfoot rk. 11 combine 2

Reward - Beast Infused Hero Stone

Armor

Leather/Plate/Chain/Silk Armor Molds can drop from any mob in the zone.

While you are killing mobs, be sure to loot the items that you will need for the combine to create the armor.

  • Primal Core of Gelid Creation (Every class needs these)

  • Primal Cloth (Cloth Classes)

  • Primal Links (Chain Classes)

  • Primal Scales (Plate Classes)

  • Primal Hide (Leather Classes)

For this example I'm going to make the Shadowknight BP. First, I'll need to visit Velgrath Ironhide to buy a Crest of the Soulrender.

Combine the following in a Universal Assembly Crate:

  • Crest of the Soulrender

  • Primal Scale

  • Plate Chest Mold of the Gelid

  • Primal Core of Gelid Creation x2 (These can not be stacked)
