Tier 3 Gear - Najena

Updated from eqprofusion.orgLast updated 2026-10-14

Progression Task “Found the Hidden Najena”

Task Giver: Grendal in Nexus or PoK

Zone Short Name: najena

Create Instance: #createdz najena

You will need to loot the following pages.

  • Torn Page of Mastery Fire

  • Torn Page of Mastery Wind

  • Torn Page of Mastery Earth

  • Torn Page of Mastery Water

Deliver all 4 pages of Borat in Najena (He is at the zone in)

  • Hand all 4 pages to him at the same time.

Moving onward - Tier 4 - The Hole

Kill Points etc…

Killing mobs in this zone will give you kill points to buy things from Borat.

Hail Borat at the zone in of Najena and you can explore those options of what you would like to buy.

- Progression Pages

- Augments

- Weapons

Accessory

    - Flower Power (4 second, 35000 heal)****

    - Resurrection Stone  (Clicky Divine Rez)

    - Call of the Hero Stone (CoH someone!)

I highly recommend the Flower Power item. It is the same as the cleric's heal spell but casting time is shorter.

Najena Kill Points can only be spent in this zone.

Astrial Major Infuse Augmentation

While you are making your way through this tier, you will want to collect the Splinters of Astrial to make this augment. This will be 1 of the augments that you will use on your Astrial Weapon.

Collect:

Bottom Splinter of Astrial

Center Splinter of Astrial

Left Splinter of Astrial

Right Splinter of Astrial

Top Splinter of Astrial

Combine these items in Plane of Knowledge using a Forge.

Reward:

Astrial's Major Infuse
