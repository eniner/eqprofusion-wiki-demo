Tier 4 Gear - The Hole

Updated from eqprofusion.orgLast updated 2026-10-14

Progression - “Get Stoned in the Hole”

The progression items drop from any mob in the zone.

  • First Piece of Underfoot Stone

  • Second Piece of Underfoot Stone

  • Third Piece of Underfoot Stone

  • Fourth Piece of Underfoot Stone

  • Fifth Piece of Underfoot Stone

  • Sixth Piece of Underfoot Stone

You can buy progression stones from Borat with kill points from The Hole.

Once you have all 6 pieces, combine them in a Universal Assembly Crate.

  • Give the item that you created to Borat in The Hole to finish the task.
