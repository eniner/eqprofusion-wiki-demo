Epics Overview (1.0 - 5.0)

New from eqprofusion.orgLast updated 2026-10-14

Epic 1.0

  • Pick up the quest Crushing Crushbone! from Mel Gibson in Nexus

Go to Crushbone to kill

- Orc Trainer

- Emperor Crush

- Slavemaster.

Turn into Mel Gibson once completed!

Epic 1.5

  • Pick up the quest One Bad Mother Guk-er! from Mel Gibson in Nexus

Go to Lower Guk to kill

- Ghoul Assassin

- Ghoul Savant

- Ghoul Sage

- Ghoul Executioner

- Ghoul Arch Magus

- Ghoul Lord

- Rastor of Guk (Rastor spawns on the live side of Lower Guk)

Turn into Mel Gibson once completed!

Note: Mobs DO have place holders so you will need to clear before some of these mobs will spawn or drop the DZ and get a new one.

  • Pick up the quest Ugh… frogs again?!?! from Grendel in Nexus

Go to Sebilis to kill

- froglok armorer

- froglok armsman

- froglok chef

- frenzied pox scarab

- Gruplinort

- Brogg

Hail/Turn in to Grendel in Nexus

Note: Mobs DO have place holders so you will need to clear before some of these mobs will spawn or drop the DZ and get a new one.

Epic 2.0 Augment

2.0 is tied in with T3 progression

Note - If you want a 2hand Epic Enhancement aug, you will need to give Borat (in Najena) two of the Rk 2 Enhancement augs.

Epic 3.0

Note - Kerafrym in Sleeper's Tomb does drop the completed Epic 3.0's in case you skip this step and you are able to kill him

First off head to Plane of Knowledge, and talk to Gilgamesh. Pick up the task from him (for tracking the quest items/locations).

You will need 8 items in total to get your new epic weapon.

  • Tome of Power: Veeshan (Tier 1: Temple Veeshan)

  • Tome of Power: Riftseekers (Tier 2: Riftseeker's Sanctum)

  • Tome of Power: Najena (Tier 3: Najena)

  • Tome of Power: Hole (Tier 4: Hole)

  • Tome of Power: MPG (Tier 5:: Muramite Proving Grounds)

  • Tome of Power: Kael (Tier 6:: Kael)

You can obtain these books by slaughtering everything you see in the appropriate zone.

Next you will need:

Go to Najena and Kill Najena (This is a T6 fight) to loot -

  • Head of Najena

Go To Unrest and kill Serra (Assuming this is T6 as well) to loot -

  • Serra's Codex

Combine all the items in your Universal Assembly Crate, and turn the Book of Epic Power - 3.0 in to Gilgamesh in Plane of Knowledge.

Rewards

  • Aurora, the Heartwood Blade 3.0 (Ranger)

  • Blade of Vesagran 3.0 (Bard)

  • Blessed Spiritstaff of the Heyokah 3.0 (Shaman)

  • Deathwhisper 3.0 (Necromancer)

  • Focus of Primal Elements 3.0 (Magician)

  • Hammer of Superior Divinity 3.0 (Cleric)

  • Innoruuk's Dark Blessing 3.0 (Shadow Knight)

  • Kreljnok's Sword of Eternal Power 3.0 (Warrior)

  • Nightbane, Sword of the Valiant 3.0 (Paladin)

  • Spiritcaller Totem of the Feral 3.0 (Beastlord)

  • Staff of Eternal Eloquence 3.0 (Enchanter)

  • Staff of Everliving Brambles 3.0 (Druid)

  • Staff of Phenomenal Power 3.0 (Wizard)

  • Transcended Fistwraps of Immortality 3.0 (Monk)

  • Vengeful Taelosian Blood Axe 3.0 (Berserker)

Epic 3.0 Augment

IMPORTANT, You cannot hand in a (two Hand) version and get a 3.0 aug upgrade you must have 2 separate pieces and twice the medals

  • Red Kromrif Champions Medal

  • Green Kromrif Champions Medal

  • Blue Kromrif Champions Medal

Random drop from giants in Kael. (The medal's are tradeable)

  • Epic Enhancement Rk. 2 (Quest from Borat in Najena)

Turn in all 4 items to Borat in Kael to receive your new Epic Enhancement Rk. 3

To get the 2hand version aug -

  • You will need to hand him two of the Epic Enhancement Rk. 3 to get Epic Enhancement Rk. 3 (Two Hand)

Epic 6.0 Augment

Quest Name: Epic 6.0 Augmentation

Quest NPC: Borat

Quest Location: Valley of King Xorbb

Borat is located in Valley of King Xorbb. He wants you to collect Book of Souls, Book of Prayers, and Book of Darkness. Click the "Combine" text when you collect all three items. All three are random and rare drops throughout the zone.

Quest dialogue is as follows:

You say, 'Hail, Borat'

Borat whispers, 'Hi there YOURNAMEHERE do you want to browse my items or check your balance?'

=============

[Spells] || [Clicks] || [Augs]

Click on [Augs]

Borat whispers, 'Hi YOURNAMEHERE, Help me find his items scattered to the four corners'

To make Epic Enhancement Range Rk. 6 we will need: Show

To make Epic Enhancement Rk. 6 (Two Hand) we will need: Show

Click on Show to have a popup window with further dialogue.
