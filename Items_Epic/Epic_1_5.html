Epic 1.5

Last updated 2020-04-25

Pick up quest from Mel Gibson before killing in Lower Guk to receive credit!

Epic 1.5 (Level 25 required to accept)-Completed in Lower Guk by killing Ghoul Assasin, Ghoul Savant, Ghoul Sage, Ghoul Executioner, Ghoul Arch Magus, Ghoul Lord and Rastor of Guk (Rastor spawns on the live side of Lower Guk)

Note: Mobs DO have place holders so you will need to clear before somme of these mobs will spawn!

Turn in to Mel Gibson once completed!
