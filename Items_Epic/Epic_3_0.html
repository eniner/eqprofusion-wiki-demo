Epic 3.0

Last updated 2020-04-25

First off head to Plane of Knowledge, and talk to Gilgamesh. Pick up the task from him (for tracking the quest items/locations).

You will need 8 items in total to get your new epic weapon.

  • Tome of Power: Veeshan (Tier 1: Temple Veeshan)
  • Tome of Power: Riftseekers (Tier 2: Riftseeker's Sanctum)
  • Tome of Power: Najena (Tier 3: Najena)
  • Tome of Power: Hole (Tier 4: Hole)
  • Tome of Power: MPG (Tier 5:: Muramite Proving Grounds)
  • Tome of Power: Kael (Tier 6:: Kael)

You can obtain these books by slaughtering everything you see in the appropriate zone.

Next you will need:

  • Najena

This is a Tier 6 fight.

  • Serra's Codex

Assuming this is a Tier 6 fight as well.

Combine all the items in your Universal Assembly Crate, and turn the Book of Epic Power - 3.0 in to Gilgamesh in Plane of Knowledge.

Rewards

  • Aurora, the Heartwood Blade 3.0 (Ranger)
  • Blade of Vesagran 3.0 (Bard)
  • Blessed Spiritstaff of the Heyokah 3.0 (Shaman)

  • Hammer of Superior Divinity 3.0 (Cleric)
  • Innoruuk's Dark Blessing 3.0 (Shadow Knight)
  • Kreljnok's Sword of Eternal Power 3.0 (Warrior)
  • Nightbane, Sword of the Valiant 3.0 (Paladin)
