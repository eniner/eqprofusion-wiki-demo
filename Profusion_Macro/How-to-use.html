How to use

Updated from eqprofusion.orgLast updated 2026-10-14

Starting / Restart / Stop the bot macro

/start - to start macro on all boots /restart - to restart quickly the macro /endall or /all end - to stop macro on all boots /end - to stop macro on the current bot /all camp desktop camp all bot and go to your desktop

Manually

/macro profusion/start

Commands to manage your bots

The phylosophie

You can ask all your bot to perform an action, a group, a type of class, or even just the current bot To do that you run a WHO command like this for exemple

/me or /all etc...

After this main command you need to add the action you need to perform.

an auto attack sequence atk or run debuff on your target debuff So you do to engage all your bots on the target /all atk

Or if you enagage a named, want to take aggro with your tank, debuff and after engage dps /me tank - /all debuff and after a delay have take the aggro, /all atk or you can engage separately your dps like this /all pet atk - /all sup - /all melee - /all nuke

How To use warp

By using a easy npc click on the map

  • Ctl + right clic - warp all your bots
  • Shiff + right clic - warp your char

To use warp from command can use :

  • To teleport self to target /warp loc ${Target.Y} ${Target.X} ${Target.Z}
  • To teleport all bots and self /bcaa //warp loc ${Target.Y} ${Target.X} ${Target.Z}
